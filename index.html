Post-Compilation Masterclass

How to Sign and Install Your APK File

Because AppShunya outputs an optimized unsigned binary for security, you must sign the APK prior to device installation. Follow these 4 easy steps:

01
Step A

Get an APK Signer Utility

Download any trusted APK signer application directly from the Google Play Store onto your device.

02
Step B

Select Unsigned APK

Open your signer app, tap **"Sign APK"**, and browse your phone storage to select the downloaded unsigned binary generated by AppShunya.

03
Step C

Apply Test Key & Sign

Choose the default test keystore (auto-sign) profile and tap the **"Sign"** button. Your secure signed APK package will be ready instantly.

04
Step D

Install and Launch

Tap the newly signed APK file to install it directly on your Android device. Your custom web application will launch seamlessly from the homescreen!
